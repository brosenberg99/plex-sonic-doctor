Plex Sonic Doctor
Welcome. First, connect to your Plex server.

Scan

Scanning only reads your library. The first scan fingerprints the candidate files, which can take about an hour over a network share; later scans only check new or changed files.


      

      

        Optional: full damage check (slow)
        
Decodes every file looking for corruption. It takes hours for a big library, saves as it goes,
          and can be stopped and resumed. Afterwards, use Quick re-sort to add what it found to Possibly broken.

      (lossless first, then higher bitrate, then proper tags, never a broken copy). Pick Remove for copies you
      don't want; nothing moves until you go to Move & undo.

    

      
      
      
      
      
    

    

      
      
      
    

    

    

  


  
  

    
Each card is one song with more than one version (an edit, a remaster, a mono mix, a live take...).
      Each row is the best copy of that version; identical copies of it are in Identical copies. Keep the
      versions you want. A song always keeps at least one version.

    

      
      
      
    

    

      

    

    

  


  
  

    
Files across the whole library that look damaged or wrong, for replacing. Broken files decode
      to garbage, won't open or stop early. Likely broken covers songs that measure as pure silence, and albums
      where every track is the same clip. Check covers constant noise (often real noise music), intentional
      silence and missing files.

    

      
      
      
    

    

    

    

  


  
  

    
Pairs whose audio only partly matches: alternate takes, remixes, variants, or the same song
      with a different intro. They aren't treated as copies. Listen if you're curious.

    

    

    

    

  


  
  

    

      
Move the copies marked for removal

      
Marked copies are moved (never deleted) to the holding folder, keeping their folder layout,
        so every move can be undone. Every song keeps at least one copy; groups where everything is marked are skipped.

      
      

      

    

    

      
Moved batches

      

      

      
Plex marks moved tracks unavailable after a rescan. When you're happy, you can delete the
        holding folder yourself. Until then, everything can be put back.

    

  


  
  

    

      
What counts as an identical copy

      
A copy is identical to the keeper only if it passes all of these checks. Anything else that
        matches the song is a different version. After changing these, use Quick re-sort.
